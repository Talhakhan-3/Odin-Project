Facts About Wolf !

Adult wolves have 42 teeth, including long canine teeth used to grip prey.

Wolves communicate through howls, body language, and scent marking.

Wolves stay active during winter and do not hibernate.

Wild wolf packs are usually family groups, made up of parents and their offspring

When the snows fall and the white winds blow, the lone wolf dies but the pack survives.
